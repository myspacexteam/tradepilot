# TradePilot 2.9.2 — Stateful Trade Timeline

- Restored one selected timeframe at a time: D1, H4, H1, M15 or M5.
- Added a fourth TRADE TIMELINE lane below the three indicator lanes.
- BUY opens only when MACD regime, closed-candle MACD state and Candle Pressure
  all align bullish (3/3).
- SELL opens only when all three align bearish (3/3).
- An open trade remains continuous across candles.
- BUY closes when fewer than two indicators still support bullish direction.
- SELL closes when fewer than two indicators still support bearish direction.
- WAIT fills periods with no simulated open trade.
- Entry triangles and close X markers show the exact open/close candles.

The timeline is an analytical simulation and does not send orders.
